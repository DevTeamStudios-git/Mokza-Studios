# Mokza Studio

**Independent Game Studio | Creating Games Worth Remembering**

Welcome to **Mokza Studio**, an independent game studio focused on creating original, engaging, and memorable gaming experiences.

Formerly known as **DTS (Developpement Team Studios)**, we are entering a new chapter with a new name, a new visual identity, and the same ambition to build games that players love.

## About Us

Mokza Studio is built around creativity, experimentation, and the pursuit of unique gameplay experiences. We aim to develop games that bring people together, encourage exploration, and give players something worth coming back to.

Our goal is to grow into a recognizable international game studio, starting with ambitious ideas and turning them into playable experiences.

## Our Mission

To create original games that entertain players, inspire creativity, and leave a lasting impression.

## What We Do

- **Game Development:** Designing and building interactive gaming experiences.
- **Gameplay Design:** Developing engaging mechanics and meaningful player progression.
- **Creative Direction:** Building distinctive game worlds, visual identities, and concepts.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Learning, experimenting, and refining our projects with every release.
- **Software Developments:** Designing, building, compilling, running and deploying.

## Our Rebrand

Mokza Studio represents the next chapter of our journey.

Our transition from DTS (Developpement Team Studios) to Mokza Studio introduces a new identity designed to be simple, memorable, and recognizable internationally.

While our name and visual identity have changed, our ambition remains the same: to create games worth playing and remembering.
